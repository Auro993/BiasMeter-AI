About BiasMeter AI

BiasMeter AI is an ethical AI bias detection platform developed for New Innovation and the Start Up Odisha Initiative. Our mission is to help organizations identify and mitigate biases in their AI systems across various industries.

With the increasing adoption of AI in decision-making processes, it's crucial to ensure these systems are fair, transparent, and unbiased. BiasMeter AI provides comprehensive tools to analyze, monitor, and improve the fairness of AI algorithms.

Our platform is designed to be accessible to both technical and non-technical users, providing clear insights and actionable recommendations to reduce bias and promote ethical AI practices.
